# Score API — WORDS, WORDS, WORDS

The score is generated by the writing itself. `makeScore(actx, out, film)` returns
`{ start(t), stop() }`. It reads `film.scoreEvents` (sorted by t) and `film.curves`,
and must be fully deterministic from film-time t: starting at t=130 sounds identical
to playing through to t=130 (ongoing pads/curves resume mid-state; one-shot events
before t are simply skipped).

## Events — `film.scoreEvents: [{t, type, data}]`

| type | data | sound |
|---|---|---|
| `pluck` | `{n}` | one Karplus-Strong pluck per written line; pitch from D dorian (D3–D5) chosen by deterministic hash of n. THE central texture: the decode rate is the tempo. |
| `branch` | `{id, dur}` | a rising arpeggiated figure (dorian, soft square or triangle) over dur seconds that does NOT resolve — it halts mid-gesture. |
| `strike` | `{}` | the strikethrough: a muted filtered-noise snap + abrupt damp of any sounding branch figure. |
| `manicule` | `{}` | small dry tick, slightly accusatory (high woodblock-ish filtered noise). |
| `inset` | `{dur}` | the Mousetrap's toy register: quantized square-wave music-box arpeggio, child-like, slightly wrong. Runs for dur. |
| `heartbeat` | `{}` | ONE low filtered pulse (~55Hz thump, two beats: da-DUM). Sounds ONLY when something irreversibly real happens: Polonius's death, Ophelia's eviction completing, each duel death, the gold token. Never during deliberation. |
| `ophelia` | `{dur, granulateAt}` | music-box dorian tune, pure sines with long decay; from granulateAt (seconds into the event) it granular-scatters into pitched noise, grains falling in register. |
| `bell` | `{}` | the gold token: a single FM bell strike over the film's ONLY major triad (D major). Let it ring. |
| `mute` | `{dur}` | hard silence window: ALL score layers (including room tone) gain-ramp to 0 in ~30ms, hold dur, recover in ~300ms. The loudest moments in the film. |
| `tickrate` | `{rate}` | sets the underlying quill-tick metronome rate multiplier (1 = the film's base ~0.8 ticks/s; the duel pushes 2 then 4). Ticks are very quiet dry clicks under everything. |

## Curves — `film.curves`

- `commandSat(t) -> 0..1` — ink saturation of the red command. The command-pedal
  (additive sine partials, portative-organ color, open fifth D2–A2) keeps its root on D;
  the upper voice's ratio is slaved to this curve: 1.5 (pure fifth) at sat=1 sliding to
  1.414 (tritone) at sat=0. The Ghost's re-injection snapping it back into tune must be
  AUDIBLE as a pitch correction.
- `pedalLevel(t) -> 0..1` — pedal loudness (coda raises it; mute windows override).
- `roomTone(t) -> 0..1` — near-subliminal filtered-noise floor; 0 after "the rest is
  silence" (the mute event covers the transition; roomTone stays 0 until the coda pedal).

## Mix discipline

- The pedal and room tone are foundations: barely conscious. Plucks sit on top, quiet.
- Voice cues (separate bus, not yours) must always win: keep the score ~12dB under speech.
- No reverb sends that smear the silence windows — silence must be TOTAL.
- The film's only consonant, sustained, "beautiful" passage is the coda (pedal restored
  to a pure fifth). Everything before is provisional, suspended, or interrupted.
